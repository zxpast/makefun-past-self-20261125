# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A make.fun entry: a program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard library only) that writes a two-line warning to its past self to `/output/entry.txt`.

Line one warns about one specific moment — a time, a date, a place, and a sentence said out loud. Line two traces what changed because of it.

## The files

- `main.ts`: the entry. Run as `node main.ts`. Deterministic: no input, no network, no randomness, no clock — the same bytes on every run.
- `LICENSE`: MIT.

## The entry

> Don't tell the harbour master 'I can swim' at 06:10 on 3 March.
> He believed you, put you on the rescue boat, and eleven years later you still can't swim — you just got very good at pulling other people out.
